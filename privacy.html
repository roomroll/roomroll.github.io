Roomroll Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Roomroll helps you tidy the photo library on your iPhone. It is built so that your photos, and what you do with them, stay on your device.

What Roomroll collects

Nothing. Roomroll does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no automatic crash reporting and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server of its own. The developer never receives your photos, information about them or how you use the app, unless you choose to send a problem report (below).

Problem reports you choose to send

Settings → Report a Problem prepares a report to help fix a problem and shows you all of it. You can send it by email from your own Mail account or share it another way. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and you can read every line first.

A report contains:

  • App and iPhone: the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, memory, free storage, heat (thermal state) and whether Low Power Mode is on.
  • Library, as counts only: whether Photos access is full or limited; how many photos, videos, screenshots and screen recordings the library has and its total size; how many items are only in iCloud or couldn't be read; whether “Download to Analyze” is on; and how many items are in the app's Bin.
  • Purchases: whether Pro is unlocked and which plan, and how many free cleanups have been used.
  • Recent activity: a log of event names, counts, durations and error codes.
  • Reports from iOS: the crash, hang and daily performance reports iOS gives the app (MetricKit), with Photos identifiers and media file names removed.

It never contains your photos or videos, their file names or identifiers, locations, album names or anything they show.

The activity log and the reports from iOS are kept in the app's storage on your iPhone, up to a small fixed size, so a report can include them. “Delete Diagnostic Data” on the Report a Problem screen erases them, and so does deleting the app. If you send a report, it is used only to find and fix the problem, and you can ask for it to be deleted at the contact address below.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, Roomroll keeps these on your device only:

  • Analysis of your library: fingerprints used to find duplicates and look-alikes, blur and size measurements, and screenshot categories. They are made on your iPhone from your Photos library and are never uploaded.
  • Your decisions: which items you kept or deleted, so they aren't suggested again.
  • Settings and your free cleanup count: your preferences, and how many free cleanups you've used. The count is kept in the app's storage and in your iPhone's Keychain, so it survives reinstalling the app.
  • Diagnostics for problem reports: described above.
  • Trial reminder, if you ask for one: after a free trial starts, Roomroll can remind you a day before it ends. The reminder is a notification scheduled by iOS on your iPhone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Deleting the app removes its storage; the cleanup count in the Keychain stays only on your iPhone. “Clear Analysis Cache” in Settings removes the analysis.

Photos access

Roomroll asks for access to your Photos library so it can find items for you to review. You can allow all photos or only the ones you choose, and change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. Hidden photos are never read.

Nothing is deleted without your confirmation in the iOS prompt, and deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days. When you compress a video, the smaller copy is saved to your library. When you file items, they are added to the albums you choose or create.

If you turn on “Download to Analyze”, iOS may download items stored only in iCloud Photos from Apple's iCloud service, under Apple's privacy policy. Roomroll itself doesn't contact any server.

Purchases

Roomroll Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether Pro is unlocked and on which plan. The developer receives no name, email address or payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Roomroll collects no data from anyone, including children. A problem report reaches the developer only if the person using the iPhone sends it.
