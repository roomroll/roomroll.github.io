Roomroll Support

Roomroll finds duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots and large videos in your iPhone's photo library, so you can review them and free up space. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Need help? Email roomroll.help@icloud.com.

In the app, Settings → Report a Problem prepares a report you can read before sending. It never includes your photos.

Common questions

Is Roomroll free?

You can use every feature for your first 200 cleanups (each item you delete or compress counts as one). After that, Roomroll Pro unlocks unlimited cleanups: yearly with a 3-day free trial, monthly, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

How do I upgrade, restore or cancel?

Upgrade in Settings → Upgrade to Pro. On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the upgrade screen. To cancel a subscription, use Settings → Manage Subscription in the app, or Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your iPhone, at least 24 hours before it renews.

Can I get deleted photos back?

Yes. Roomroll deletes only what you confirm in the iOS prompt, and deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Why doesn't it see all my photos?

If you allowed access to selected photos only, Roomroll sees just those. Choose more from the app's home screen, or allow full access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. Hidden photos are never read. Items stored only in iCloud are skipped unless you turn on Download to Analyze in the app's Settings.

Do my photos leave my iPhone?

No. Roomroll analyzes your library on your iPhone and has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. See the privacy policy.

Does compressing a video replace the original?

No. Roomroll saves a smaller copy to your library and puts the original in the app's Bin. Nothing is deleted until you empty the Bin and confirm in the iOS prompt, and you can keep the original instead.
